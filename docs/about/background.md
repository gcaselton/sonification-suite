
Sonification is the process of communicating data through sound.

Our first domain, the Sonification Suite for Planetaria, aims to provide a set of sonification tools for science communicators and planetarium content creators, so that they can use sound to make their astronomy shows more immersive and accessible.

The genesis of the project was the *Audio Universe: Tour of the Solar System* show, a full-dome planetarium show which explored our solar system using both sonification and traditional planetarium visuals. The feedback was positive from both visually impaired and sighted audiences alike, leading to the motivation to build a toolkit which science communicators could use to create their own sonifications for use in their besopke content.
